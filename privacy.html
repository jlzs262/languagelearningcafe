1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to Learn German Cafe (Das Deutsche Sprachcafé Wort & Waffel) as an Apple iOS app and an Android app, as well as to its informational website. It explains how personal data is handled when you play the game or visit the website.

The person or business responsible for the apps and this website is the operator identified in the Impressum. You can contact the operator using the details provided there.

Apple App Store

The iOS version of Learn German Cafe is covered by this Privacy Policy. The data that is collected or transmitted includes account details such as gameplay information, scores of the player, username and email in order to be able to save the progress of the user and being able to transmit it on different versions of the game such as the desktop or mobile version. Storage without a saved username or email is stored locally until the session is refreshed. Data with a saved email for the user goes to a database in order to secure the data, and not have personal information such as the user's email to stay on the client-side and be readable. Third-party services that the app uses include Supabase for database management and VoiceCafe TTS hosted on Cloudflare for vocabulary pronunciation.

Google Play

The Android version of Learn German Cafe is covered by this Privacy Policy. The data that is collected or transmitted includes account details such as gameplay information, scores of the player, username and email in order to be able to save the progress of the user and being able to transmit it on different versions of the game such as the desktop or mobile version. Storage without a saved username or email is stored locally until the session is refreshed. Data with a saved email for the user goes to a database in order to secure the data, and not have personal information such as the user's email to stay on the client-side and be readable. Third-party services that the app uses include Supabase for database management and VoiceCafe TTS hosted on Cloudflare for vocabulary pronunciation.

The informational website is hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages. To deliver and secure the site, GitHub processes technical connection data, including the visitor’s IP address, request time, requested page or file, and browser or device information. The legal basis for this security-related processing is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. GitHub’s documentation explains its data practices, including possible processing outside the European Economic Area: see the GitHub Pages data collection documentation and the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Websites such as itch.io, Bluesky, Instagram, Threads, and other social media third-party platforms have their own privacy policies and data practices. Their independent processing is not covered by this policy.

2. Our data promise

We will never sell your personal data. We do not use personal data for advertising or tracking.

The website itself does not set analytics or advertising cookies, use tracking pixels, create visitor profiles, or store information in your browser’s local storage. It does not contain a contact form, account system, or embedded social media feed. The embedded itch.io trial is third-party content; its data practices are described below.

3. Fonts, images, embedded trial, and links

The website’s own fonts, images, stylesheets, and scripts are served locally from the same GitHub Pages site. Simply browsing the website does not establish a connection to Google Fonts or social media platforms.

The homepage includes a playable trial embedded from itch.io. The embed loads lazily as you approach the trial section. When it loads, your browser connects to itch.io, which may receive technical connection data such as your IP address and browser or device information and may use cookies or similar storage. We do not control itch.io’s processing; its own privacy policy applies.

Links to Bluesky, Instagram, Threads, and other third-party websites are ordinary external links. A connection to the relevant provider is established only when you choose to open one of those links. The provider’s own privacy policy then applies.

4. Contact enquiries

If you contact the website operator directly, for example by email, the information you provide will be processed to answer your enquiry and for any resulting follow-up. This may include your email address, name, message, and any other information you choose to share.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR when the enquiry concerns a contract or potential contract, and otherwise Article 6(1)(f) GDPR based on the legitimate interest in responding to enquiries. Enquiry records are retained only as long as necessary for these purposes or to meet applicable legal obligations.

5. Your data protection rights

Where the GDPR applies, you have the right to request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion, restriction of processing, and data portability. You may also object to processing based on legitimate interests, including on grounds relating to your particular situation.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority. To exercise your rights or ask a question about this policy, contact the operator using the details in the Impressum.

6. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, hosting arrangement, or applicable legal requirements change. The current version and its revision date will always be published on this page.
